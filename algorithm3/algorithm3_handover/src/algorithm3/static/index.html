Algorithm 3 demo: voice interaction and audio monitoring

Everything runs on this machine. Audio is processed in memory only, never saved or uploaded. This page only shows the calls of the algorithm.

Connecting...

1. Sound-event monitoring

A recording goes through the front end (two gates), the YAMNet embedding and a logistic regression and comes out as HELP / IMPACT / ABNORMAL / DAILY / UNKNOWN. The label is only a hint: it never raises or cancels an alarm.

2. Reply recognition ("What's wrong? Are you OK?")

Speech recognition (Whisper tiny) plus reply rules v2 give HELP / REASSURANCE / OTHER / UNCERTAIN. Without an enrolled voiceprint, "I'm fine" is judged by its words and the recogniser confidence only.

3. Spoken call for help

Simulates continuous listening: the speech gate cuts out speech segments, and only a segment of 0.3 to 6 s with enough recogniser confidence that is a clear call for help raises an alarm, at most once in 30 s.

4. Alarm decision and channel tag

The sensor candidates and the result of the voice inquiry decide whether the event is escalated, and a fixed 10 s window gives the single / dual / triple channel tag. These are plain rules, no model is needed.
